1. Open the beta Hawkes Net demo and Join.
2. Walk into an edge wall, then turn so your back is to it: the camera should pull in smoothly and never clip through the wall.
3. Stand still for about 2 seconds: the camera eases back behind you.
4. On a phone (or `?touch=1`), drag on the right half of the screen to orbit; the left half still steers the stick.
